Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is the minimum order quantity for wiring harnesses?

We don't have a strict MOQ for custom harnesses. For standard products, MOQ varies by type - typically 50-100 pieces for automotive harnesses. Contact us for specific requirements.

Do you provide design support for custom harnesses?

Yes, our engineering team provides complete design support including schematic creation, BOM optimization, connector selection, and DFM analysis. This is included in our quotation process.

What testing do you perform on harnesses?

100% continuity testing, hipot testing (up to 5kV), insulation resistance, pull force verification, visual inspection, and dimensional checks. We can also perform environmental testing per customer requirements.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms: 30% advance with order, 70% before dispatch. For repeat customers, we offer net 30/45 days. LC and export payment terms available for international orders.

Can you handle EV high-voltage harnesses?

Yes, we have a dedicated EV division with cleanroom assembly for high-voltage harnesses (400V-800V). We use ultrasonic welding, shielded cables, and follow AIS-038/LV standards for EV safety.

What is the typical lead time?

Prototypes: 2-3 weeks. Production orders: 4-6 weeks depending on complexity and volume. We maintain safety stock for common connectors and wires to reduce lead times.
