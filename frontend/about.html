About Segan Industry

Precision engineering meets AI-powered automation since 2009

Our Story

Founded in 2009 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Segan Industry Private Limited began with a vision to become the most trusted wiring harness manufacturer in South India. Over 15 years, we've evolved from a small workshop to a full-scale manufacturing facility serving automotive OEMs, EV startups, and industrial automation companies.

Our journey has been defined by continuous innovation. In 2018, we achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification. In 2021, we invested in AI-powered automation for quotation generation, workflow optimization, and inventory management. Today, we're pursuing IATF 16949 certification to meet global automotive standards.

Located in the SIDCO Industrial Estate, Ambattur - Chennai's manufacturing hub - we benefit from excellent infrastructure, skilled workforce, and proximity to major ports for export operations.
